FAQ

 

How do I add it to my server?

Click “Add to Discord”, pick your server, and approve the permissions. Then join a voice channel and run /stations browse.

What permissions does it need?

Connect and Speak in voice, plus Send Messages and Embed Links so it can show what's playing.

Can I cancel Premium any time?

Yes. Cancel whenever you like and your server drops back to the free plan.
