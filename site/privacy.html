Who we are

Crüe FM is a Discord bot run by [your name or company]. Contact: [contact email].

What we collect

We do not read your messages, and the bot does not use Discord's message content intent. We store only what the radio needs to work:

  • Server settings: your server's ID, the voice and text channel IDs the radio uses, the station playing, its volume, and the ID of the person who started it.
  • Control panel: the channel and message IDs of the radio panel, so it can update itself.
  • Your area (optional): your Discord user ID and the state/city you last picked, so the station picker can offer it next time. Run /stations forget to delete it.
  • Favorites: the stations a server saved.
  • Premium themes: a server's chosen radio name, colour, tagline and logo image. Logos are shrunk and kept only as a small PNG.
  • Subscription status: Discord tells us which servers have an active Premium subscription (server ID and plan). Payments are handled by Discord; we never see card details.
  • Logs: short operational logs (errors, which station failed) that may include server names or IDs. They are used only to run and fix the bot.

How long we keep it

When the bot is removed from a server, we delete that server's saved radio, panel, favorites and theme. Your remembered area stays until you run /stations forget. Logs are kept for a short time by our hosting provider.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. The bot runs on Fly.io and talks to Discord. To find and play stations it contacts the Radio Browser station directory, the radio stations' own streams, and (for free music) ccMixter and Jamendo. Those services see the bot's requests, not your Discord identity.

Your choices

Remove the bot to delete a server's data, use /stations forget for your area, or contact us at [contact email] to ask for anything else to be deleted.

Children

Discord requires users to be at least 13 (or the minimum age in their country). We do not knowingly collect data from younger users.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above.
